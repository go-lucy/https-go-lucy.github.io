01 / The declaration
We believe the software factory is ending. The people closest to a problem should be able to shape its solution. Everyone creates. Everything evolves.

We don't pretend to have all the answers. We are committed to challenging the old assumptions, trying new approaches, and helping discover what comes next.

We are putting our names behind that future.
